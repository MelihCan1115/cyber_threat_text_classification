Accuracy Improvement Strategies

Evidence-based techniques to push performance higher

High Impact

Increase LoRA Rank

Upgrade from r=8 to r=16 or r=32. Higher rank = more learnable parameters = better task adaptation without much extra VRAM on an A100.

High Impact

Increase Epochs

Currently 3 epochs. Pushing to 5–6 epochs with early stopping on macro_f1 allows the model to converge more completely, especially for minority classes.

High Impact

Weighted Loss for Class Imbalance

The dominant-label approach creates class imbalance. A custom Trainer with class_weight='balanced' from sklearn (computed from training labels) can significantly boost minority class F1.

Medium Impact

Extend Max Token Length

Current max_len=128 may truncate cybersecurity reports. Increasing to 256 or 512 lets the model see full context, especially for long threat descriptions.

Medium Impact

Lower Learning Rate + Cosine Schedule

Try lr=1e-4 instead of 2e-4 with a cosine annealing schedule. Prevents overshooting and helps fine models like Gemma-2 and Qwen stabilize.

Medium Impact

Add Secondary Dataset

Augment training with AI4Sec/cti-bench or kasai/cti-reports mapped to the same label space. More diverse cyber text improves generalization and cross-lingual transfer.

Low-Cost Quick Win

Label Smoothing

Add label_smoothing_factor=0.1 to TrainingArguments. Prevents overconfidence on noisy NER-derived labels at near-zero extra cost.

Low-Cost Quick Win

Gradient Checkpointing

Enable gradient_checkpointing=True to free VRAM and allow larger batches, improving training throughput without hurting accuracy.
